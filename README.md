# OFF GRID · ऑफ़ ग्रिड

> **The pause is the product.** / **अंतराल ही उत्पाद है।**  
> *RAP MEDIA · OFF GRID · BRAND SYSTEM v1*  
> *16:00 — 19:00 IST · Bengaluru Coworking Terrace*

An unhurried, interactive scrollytelling registration form for **OFF GRID**, built strictly according to the Brand System v1 specifications.

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity Specifications

### I. The Palette
- **PAPER**: `#F3EEE2` (bg · cream · default ground)
- **PAPER COOL**: `#E7E3D6` (bg · cool cream · secondary)
- **INK**: `#0E1116` (fg · body · Devanagari lead)
- **SCARLET**: `#E63323` (accent · Latin wordmark · focus moments)
- **COBALT**: `#1D3BE0` (terrace / inverse ground · golden-hour compliant)
- **SUN**: `#F6D32B` (callout · ≤ 8% composition)
- **WHITE**: `#FFFFFF` (reserved under-coat)

### II. Golden-Hour Rule (17:15 — 18:50 Local)
Between 17:15 and 18:50 Bengaluru local time, warm ambient light desaturates paper cream to mud. Graphics automatically or manually shift to **COBALT** or **INK** grounds during this interval.

### III. Typography
- **Primary**: **Anek Latin** and **Anek Devanagari** (by Ek Type) at Extra Bold weight (800) for the bilingual wordmark and headings.
- **Monospace**: **JetBrains Mono** for timestamps, notice-board registers, metadata, and analog time-blocks.

### IV. Analog Archetypes (v1)
1. `[01] The Observer` — Logged off, visible
2. `[02] Screen-Fatigued` — Strictly no display light
3. `[03] Zero-Ping Enforcer` — Slack & Teams dead
4. `[04] Silent Attendee` — Presence without performance
5. `[05] Terrace Squatter` — Cold can & fresh air
6. `[06] Off-Duty Operator` — Sanctioned unavailability

### V. Visual Guardrails
- ❌ No gradients, drop-shadows, glows, or 3D renders.
- ❌ No religious/caste-coded motifs, Ganjifa cards, or Bollywood pastiche.
- ❌ No corporate wellness clichés (`detox`, `recharge`, `mindful`, `safe space`).
- ❌ No festival slang (`mela`, `bazaar`, `rave`, `vibe`, `slay`).
- ❌ Fixed 85-LPI Riso halftone simulation at 30° screen angle.
